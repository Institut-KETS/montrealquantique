Faculty-led research across Montréal

Montréal at the heart of quantum information processing.

Montréal Quantique connects researchers and graduate students working in quantum information processing across Montréal’s universities. This faculty-led initiative makes local expertise easier for industry to discover, helps students find courses across institutional boundaries, and supports exchange through a shared programme of talks and research visits.
