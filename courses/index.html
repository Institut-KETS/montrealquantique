Courses across Montréal

This catalogue focuses on graduate and research-level courses in quantum information processing across Montréal. Selected undergraduate courses are also listed when they provide a useful pathway into the field. Each record links to the host institution, while current availability remains subject to the official schedule.Ce catalogue porte principalement sur les cours des cycles supérieurs et de niveau recherche en traitement de l’information quantique à Montréal. Certains cours de premier cycle sont aussi répertoriés lorsqu’ils constituent une voie d’accès utile au domaine. Chaque fiche renvoie à l’établissement d’accueil; l’offre actuelle demeure assujettie à l’horaire officiel.

Cross-university study

The Bureau de coopération interuniversitaire’s AEHE framework can support study outside a home institution for regular students at Québec universities. A request proceeds through the home programme, the home registrar, and the host registrar; institutional rules still apply, and the host institution may refuse registration. This catalogue supports discovery and does not guarantee eligibility, registration, or credit approval.

Read the current BCI overview and application steps, consult the official agreement, and verify the current process with both institutions. Links checked 3 October 2026.
