Proposed governance · 13 October 2026

Montréal Quantique governance

Status: the structure and all named roles below are proposed, pending adoption, acceptance and institutional confirmation. Seats marked vacant remain unfilled. This page does not establish appointments or delegated authority.

Proposed purpose

Goals of Montréal Quantique

Montréal Quantique is proposed as a community initiative with the following goals:

  1. Improve visibility and cohesion. Make existing Montréal research in quantum information processing easier to discover and connect across institutions.
  2. Provide a useful public directory. Help industry, prospective students, researchers and other potential collaborators identify and engage principal investigators affiliated with Montréal-based universities, institutes and centres.
  3. Strengthen research collaboration. Support new and continuing quantum information processing research through collaborative visits, talks, shared activities and connections among local teams.
  4. Connect education across Montréal. Improve the visibility of research-level courses and help students find relevant expertise and learning opportunities across institutional boundaries.
  5. Develop into a community research hub. Over time, and subject to community adoption and available resources, provide a shared point of connection for people, institutions, research opportunities and collaborative quantum software work in Montréal.
  6. Cultivate a globally leading research programme. Develop a coordinated Montréal-wide programme with the long-term ambition of making Montréal a global leader in quantum information processing, particularly in quantum applications science. Connect foundational research, algorithms, software and application-driven collaboration across institutions.

A community, not a membership body

Montréal Quantique brings together graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, principal investigators and professors affiliated with Montréal institutions active in quantum science, engineering, technology and education. It is an open community, with no membership roll or dues. Participation does not depend on an institution filling a committee seat.

Community participation

An annual General Assembly would provide an open forum for eligible community participants, with one person, one vote. Committees would document decisions, disclose conflicts of interest, share privacy-safe summaries and review their work annually. Vacancies would be filled through the relevant institutional, club or student appointment process.

Proposed committees

People and open seats

Strategic Advisory Committee

The committee would guide the community's priorities, review its activities and support collaboration among participating institutions.

  1. Jean Belzile (École de technologie supérieure — ÉTS), proposed Chair and institutional representative.
  2. McGill University representative — vacant.
  3. Concordia University representative — vacant.
  4. Université de Montréal representative — vacant.
  5. Polytechnique Montréal representative — vacant.
  6. Student representative elected by the Student Committee — vacant until elected.

Jacob Biamonte (École de technologie supérieure — ÉTS) is the proposed Scientific Spokesperson, reporting to this committee. The spokesperson role carries no additional institutional vote. Each represented institution has one vote, with one additional vote for the elected student representative.

Speakers and Visitors Selection Committee

The committee would select visiting speakers and coordinate research visits and talks across Montréal.

  1. Brigitte Pientka (McGill University), proposed Chair.
  2. Charles-Alexandre Bédard (École de technologie supérieure — ÉTS), proposed member.
  3. Ryan Kavanagh (Université du Québec à Montréal), proposed member.
  4. Concordia University representative — vacant.
  5. Université de Montréal representative — vacant.
  6. Polytechnique Montréal representative — vacant.
  7. Student representative elected by the Student Committee — vacant until elected.

Each represented institution has one vote, with one additional vote for the elected student representative; chairing the committee confers no extra vote. Selection would balance institutional rotation, disciplinary breadth, career stages and disclosed conflicts of interest.

Visitors willing to stay at least two weeks and pursue collaboration with Montréal Quantique members are preferred. For stays longer than two weeks, visitors are expected to deliver at least one publicly accessible research seminar per week during the extended stay. These expectations guide programme selection and visit planning.

Quantum Education Planning Committee

The committee would coordinate Montréal-wide quantum education planning, improve the visibility of research-level courses, support cross-institutional study and identify opportunities for shared teaching activities.

  1. Olivier Landon-Cardinal (École de technologie supérieure — ÉTS), proposed Chair.
  2. Nicolás Quesada (Polytechnique Montréal), proposed Co-chair.
  3. Additional institutional representatives (2–3 positions) — vacant.
  4. Student representative elected by the Student Committee — vacant until elected.

Each represented institution would have one vote, with one additional vote for the elected student representative. The Chair and Co-chair would carry no additional vote. Appointments remain proposed pending acceptance and the required institutional confirmations.

Student Committee

The committee would organize rotating student-led club events at each partner location, with each participating club leading an activity in the annual programme.

  1. Pacôme Gasnier, proposed Chair.
  2. One representative from each participating student club — vacancies filled by the clubs.
  3. The committee's elected representative to the other governing committees — vacant until elected.

All eligible graduate students may participate and vote, whether or not they belong to a club. The elected representative would hold one vote on each other governing committee. Organizing roles confer no extra vote within the Student Committee.

Webpage Committee

The proposed committee would maintain the Montréal Quantique website and support accurate, accessible public information in French and English.

  1. Jacob Biamonte, proposed member.
  2. Dmitry Guskov, proposed member.
  3. Michael J. McGuffin, proposed member.
  4. Open position — vacant.
  5. Open position — vacant.

No chair is proposed; coordination would be shared among the committee’s members.

Adoption and accountability

The proposed structure would take effect only after the required community and institutional confirmations. Committee records would distinguish proposed, accepted and confirmed roles, and public summaries would protect personal and internal information.
