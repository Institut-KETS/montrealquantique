Montréal Quantique collaborates with INTRIQ to support the Speakers & Visitors programme. Several members of our community also contribute to INTRIQ, including through its scientific and executive committees.

Fair rotation

The deterministic allocation divides six lead-host slots as evenly as possible. Remainders rotate between cycle years. Joint events retain one lead host for accounting.

Fixture allocations illustrate the method; they are not partner commitments.
