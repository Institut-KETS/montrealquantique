Research profile

Research overview

Ryan Kavanagh develops logical and semantic foundations for programming languages, especially concurrent, communicating and quantum systems. His work studies session types, process semantics and methods that help programmers specify, implement and reason about communication safely. Current publications also extend these ideas to quantum programming through a reconstruction of Proto-Quipper, linking language design, logic and mechanically checkable reasoning about programs.

Quantum programming Formal verification Quantum algorithms
