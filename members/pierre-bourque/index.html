Pierre Bourque

ProfessorProfesseur

ÉTS Montréal, University of Québec

Pierre Bourque studies software engineering as a discipline and profession, including requirements, measurement, project management, bodies of knowledge and engineering education. His recent quantum-software work examines requirements specification and functional-size measurement for quantum and hybrid classical-quantum systems. That work brings established software measurement and quality concepts to questions about how quantum software is specified, compared and managed.
