Olivier Landon-Cardinal

Teaching ProfessorProfesseur enseignant

ÉTS Montréal, University of Québec

Olivier Landon-Cardinal works in theoretical physics and quantum information, with current ÉTS expertise spanning quantum error correction, quantum characterization and quantum engineering. His teaching and supervision connect those foundations to quantum algorithms and quantum machine learning, including projects on mid-circuit measurements in QAOA and hybrid learning. Earlier research investigated robust storage of quantum information and many-body approaches to quantum memory.
