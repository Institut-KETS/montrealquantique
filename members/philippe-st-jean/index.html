Philippe St-Jean

Assistant Professor

Université de Montréal

Philippe St-Jean leads an experimental program in topological quantum photonics and light-matter interactions. His group uses optical spectroscopy and nanofabrication to create robust photonic devices, emulate unconventional quantum phases and study correlated photons and chiral quantum optics. Current projects include topological quantum walks, nonclassical light, inverse design with machine learning, quantum sensing and hardware-efficient superconducting-qubit concepts.
