Sylvain G. Cloutier

ProfessorProfesseur

ÉTS Montréal, University of Québec

Sylvain G. Cloutier develops materials and fabrication methods for optoelectronic and flexible electronic devices. His program includes nanomaterials, nanofabrication, optical microspectroscopy, printed electronics, light-emitting devices, photovoltaics and sensors, with emphasis on scalable low-cost manufacturing and structural and optoelectronic characterization. The reviewed institutional sources do not establish a current research program in quantum software or quantum information.Sylvain G.
