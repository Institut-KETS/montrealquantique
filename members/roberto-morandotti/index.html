Roberto Morandotti

Professor; Tier 1 Canada Research Chair in Smart PhotonicsProfesseur; titulaire de la Chaire de recherche du Canada de niveau 1 en photonique intelligente

Institut national de la recherche scientifique

Roberto Morandotti leads research in nonlinear, integrated, quantum and terahertz photonics. His program develops integrated frequency-comb sources for quantum optics, high-dimensional entangled states, photonic quantum-information processing and communication, mode-locked lasers and synthetic optical lattices. The work combines microfabrication, ultrafast optical experiments and nonlinear dynamics, using optical platforms both as quantum devices and as controlled analogues of quantum-mechanical effects.
