Research profile

Research overview

Frédéric Dupont-Dupuis works in quantum information and quantum computing. His Université de Montréal profile identifies quantum computing as his expertise and records research on concentration of measure for quantum states. His broader programme includes information-theoretic methods, approximation questions, and quantum protocols, including participation in the Québec–Ontario consortium on quantum protocols. He is also a member of the Centre de recherches mathématiques.

Research topics

Quantum complexity Quantum algorithms Quantum cryptography
