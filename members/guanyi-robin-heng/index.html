Guanyi (Robin) Heng

Graduate studentÉtudiant aux cycles supérieurs

ÉTS Montréal, University of Québec McGill University

Guanyi (Robin) Heng works on practical methods for preparing, characterizing, and certifying quantum states. His recent research uses effective parent Hamiltonians and local measurement data to establish fidelity bounds and certify multipartite entanglement on programmable quantum processors. The work links algorithm design, quantum simulation, and verification methods for noisy hardware.
