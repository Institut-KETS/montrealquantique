Jean-Michel Lemay

Teaching ProfessorProfesseur enseignant

ÉTS Montréal, University of Québec

Jean-Michel Lemay is a physicist and mathematician whose ÉTS profile lists expertise in quantum mechanics, symmetries and superintegrable systems, orthogonal polynomials, and algebraic structures. His work and teaching connect mathematical structures with quantum-mechanical models, including the analysis of symmetries and exactly structured systems. The reviewed public source does not establish a specific quantum-software research programme.
